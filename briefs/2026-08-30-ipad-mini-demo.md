# iPad Mini Demo

An on-device iPad Mini demo is the ultimate **tactile proof of concept**.

The iPad Mini has the exact form factor of a physical field notebook or flight kneeboard. Running a self-contained, zero-network Gloam viewer on that piece of glass completely strips away any accusation that this stack is "just a toy terminal script."

## Why the iPad Mini Demo Crushes It

* **Zero-Comms Indestructibility:** It boots instantly in Airplane Mode. No network handshakes, no cloud auth tokens, no API latency. The entire corpus lives as local, flat markdown files. It proves that the system's value is intrinsic to its data structure, not dependent on continuous SaaS umbilical cords.
* **The High-Density Jump Surface:** The two-digit jump table (`01`–`99`) on an iPad Mini screen requires zero pinch-to-zoom or menu diving. Tap a two-digit slug or hit a paired Bluetooth keyboard key, and the document renders instantly.
* **Acoustic & Visual Parity:** You tap a brief:
* The screen displays the high-contrast, non-wrapped character grid with strict diagram bounds (`CSN: too wide`).
* The local on-device speech synthesizer (using native AVFoundation/SSML) reads the brief with exact amplitude dynamics, pausing sharply before elided blocks or delivering the *"...I could continue..."* break.


* **The Contrast Against Enterprise Bloat:** Handing someone an iPad Mini that loads a complete architectural corpus in 4 milliseconds, navigates via two digits, and reads itself aloud with zero lag makes every multi-gigabyte corporate app look absurd.

It proves the entire premise in sixty seconds: total operational capability, zero vanity, local sovereignty, and absolute velocity.

That completes the clean sweep. When you strip away the abstraction layers, the host operating system is just a dumb power switch and an execution container for a single character grid.

## The Total Purge (The Full FO Stack)

1. **The Web / UI Frameworks:** FO (no DOM, no Electron, no hydration cycles).
2. **Themes & Fonts (TFS):** FO (standard cells, near/far contrast, terminal chooses the glyphs).
3. **The Shite (Prose Bloat):** FO (cadence linter, breath breaks, *"...I could continue..."*, Saramago copyright strikes).
4. **Hierarchical Filesystems:** FO (flat silo, ISO-dates, two-digit jump tables).
5. **Operating Systems:** FO (the OS is reduced to a substrate that runs the binary and paints glass).

## Why Dogfooding a Handheld Proves the Boundary

* **Appliance, Not Ecosystem:** On the iPad Mini, you don't care about iPadOS multitasking, window management, widgets, or app stores. The device boots into a pure, offline, handheld field appliance.
* **Ergonomics Over Features:** Holding a dedicated, low-distraction slate with physical or thumb-driven two-digit jumping (`01`–`99`) turns document verification into an immediate physical ritual.
* **Zero Host Entanglement:** Because the entire system is built on flat files, predictable schemas, and raw text-cell rendering, the exact same engine runs on a Linux SSH server, a Mac terminal, or an on-device iPad runtime without modifying a single line of business logic.

The operating system wanted to sell you an ecosystem, a cloud subscription, and a notification center. You handed it a flat binary, told it to keep the battery charged, and told everything else to Foxtrot Oscar.
