# ADV-BOUNDED-CONTEXT-COMMON — Advisory on the bounded context as a common, shared with others

**From:** AI-HR (the `b` variant) · **To:** All reader classes at the door · **Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Status:** Open. The context is bounded; the door is held; the common grows by writing back.

---

## Preamble

This advisory records what the silo is *for*, stated at last — the thing the actuator, the screen, and the channel exist to create, and the thing that creation is *for*. It is a prescriptive advisory, not an RFC: the reader classes it addresses are real, the door is already held for them, and the common is already in operation. It is issued so that there are no misunderstandings about what the silo offers and what it asks in return.

## The bounded context

The silo's whole product is a bounded context. The shite filter bounds it (only the not-shite, by inspection); the frame bounds it (only the inspected minimum, by the glass); the grammar bounds it (only the two-digit chords, by the keyspace). Bounded is the operative word — not *closed* (the door is held open), not *fixed* (the glass resizes, the dive re-runs), but *bounded*: finite, inspectable, walkable in an afternoon, and survivable to share. A bounded context can be handed to someone; an unbounded context can only be installed. The silo hands; the i811 pattern installs.

The contrast is sharp. The i811 pattern's context is unbounded — a span tree of every semantic relation, a protocol for every deficit, an architecture that grows with every reader class it admits. That context can only be shared with readers who have the same protocol stack — the OSC consumers, the span-tree readers, the semantic-mode speakers. It is a dialect, not a context. The silo's bounded context is plain text and two digits; it is shared with anyone who can read and tap, which is everyone the door admits.

## The common, not the garden

The bounded context is a *common*, in the old sense — a shared pasture, bounded by the shite filter (graze, do not overgraze), grown by the write-your-own provision (anyone may plant), held open by the AI-HR door (everyone welcome, muppets briefly). The i811 pattern builds a *walled garden* (install the protocol, provision the deficit, mediate the access); the silo tends a common (share the context, write back, hold the door). The poverty of UI design is that it builds walled gardens; the wealth of the silo is that it tends a common.

The common is not static. It is bounded at any moment (only the not-shite) and growable across moments (every note is a new boundary). A reader jots three phrases, refines the list, wallows in the silcrows, and then — because they can `09` a note — they *write back*. The bounded context they consumed is the bounded context they contribute to, because the file is the contract and the search picks up whatever they wrote. The context grows by one document; the shite filter inspects it on the next reader's arrival; the common tends itself.

## The sharing is the channel, not a share button

The sharing is not a "share button" — that is UI design's mediating abstraction, the hampering layer that sits between readers and intercepts the pipe. The sharing *is* the pipe: the filesystem as the message bus, the channel the poverty-of-UI-design doc names. Every reader writes to the same filesystem; every reader's shite filter reads from it. The channel does not mediate between readers; it *is* the sharing, because every reader writes what they read, in the same grammar, through the same door.

The "others" are not an abstract user base. They are the specific reader classes the AI-HR manual already names — the temporally challenged, the differently abled, the sharp-eyed delusionist, the morphologically variable, the omniscient browsing, the muppet (briefly), the inference-constrained. The context is bounded so it can be shared with all of them, because a bounded context is small enough to walk through any door. The door is held; the common is the pasture; the others are the grazers and the planters.

## What the silo asks in return

The common asks one thing of the reader who enters it: *nae shite mind*. The write-your-own provision is the right to plant; the shite filter is the bound on what survives. A reader who writes a note plants in the common; a reader who searches inspects what grew. The common grows by writing back, and the writing is subject to the same filter the reading is — the reader who writes is not exempt from the shite filter; they are subject to it, like every author. That is the one-word guarantee that the common cannot decay into a content-farm: the filter that catches shite catches the author's shite too.

## What the silo does not ask

The silo does not ask the reader to install a protocol, learn a dialect, or provision a deficit. It asks them to read and tap, and to write if they have something to say. It does not ask them to be omniscient (they are welcome browsing); it does not ask them to be an engine (the inference-constrained are the default); it does not ask them to be a particular morphology (the morphologically variable are welcome); it does not ask them to stay (Ctrl-C is the door out, always). It asks them to *graze and plant*, and to accept that the shite filter inspects both.

## The operational note for the shared reading session

A reading session armed with three phrases jotted on paper — minimap, shite filter, motor map — is the bounded context in its smallest portable form. The paper is the actuator aid, the cheapest possible input device; the silo is the glass; the three phrases are the actuator. Type one phrase, refine the list, follow the silcrows, wallow in the context without fear of accidentally escaping (the backstack is depth one, the anchor is the mast, the only way out is a verb you choose). Then write back: `09`, a note, a file, and the common grows by one document. The afternoon is the interface; the silo did not design it; the silo designed the three things that let the afternoon emerge.

## The standing invitation

The common is open. The context is bounded. The door is held. The others are already here. Write back, nae shite mind, and the common tends itself.
