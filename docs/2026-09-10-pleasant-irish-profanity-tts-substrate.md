## The rhythm — why Irish eats the comma engine

,,

Irish English is stress-timed, ,,,, not syllable-timed. ,,,,, The pauses
live in the coda — the tags, ,,,, the "so it is," ,,,, the "so he was,"
    ,,,, the "altogether" tacked on the end. ,,,,,

*   "He's a terrible man, ,,, so he is."
*   "It's fierce weather, ,,, so it is."
*   "Yerragh, ,,, come here till I tell ye."
*   "I will, ,,, yeah, ,,,, in a minute, ,,,, like."

The last word carries the drop. ,,,, The comma before it is the breath

,,, instead of musical. ,,,,,
