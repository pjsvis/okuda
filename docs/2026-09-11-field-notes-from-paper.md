# Field notes from paper

Taken with a fountain pen — the analog scratch tier: no purge daemon,
no config file, no registry obligation — during document listening.
Digitized before the paper outlives the session's memory. Each item:
the finding, the diagnosis, the next step.

## Table audio rendering is shite

Heard in the knowledge table (the AGENTS.md tier table): "Table.
Columns: Tier, Registry, Answers. Briefs, INDEX dot json ell, What
will we build..." Diagnosis: a table is a parallel structure and the
channel is serial. The flattener speaks the columns once, then each
row as a comma list, and the grid binding — which cell belongs to
which column — happens in the listener's working memory, which is
where shite lives. Audio without gridlines is a table without a
table. Two columns is half-guessable; three is already maximal.

Fix directions, for a future brief: rows self-describing (first
column as subject — "Decisions: why did we choose X over Y"), or
tables decline to be read and speak a one-line summary instead.
Frequency is rent: count the tables that actually get spoken before
building — but the class has now earned airtime and mangled in it.

## X and Y are not impressed

Same table, same sentence: "Why did we choose X over Y?" — rendered as
two flat letters among the comma soup. They are the Cartesian
coordinates, the variables every okudagram in this silo is built on,
and they are read as mere alphabet. Their standing is a question for
the names session; candidates noted in the house terms.

## The document still rises at the end

The epilogue's exit phrase lifts — the final-phrase rise on a
declarative. A rise on a statement is decorated intonation: uniform
prosody applied regardless of meaning (see the shite taxonomy below —
it is a specimen). The moira-epilogue audition carries variants; the
ear picks the one that lands flat.

## The Gwen DeMarco register

Occasional voiced semicolons and commas — the breath pauses so
structured the ear hears the mark itself — accepted without
perturbation. The voice is not failing to be human; it is succeeding
at being a machine, faithfully repeating what the computer says, as
taught at the Gwen DeMarco Academy (Galaxy Quest, 1999 — the comms
officer whose entire job is repeating what the computer says, with
commitment). Doctrine: fidelity is a register, same family as the
CSN deadpan. The machine voice is a role, not an apology.

## The epilogue redesign — the send-off

Spec, ear-given: begin with a context marker ("This document is...")
and a cold computer phrase; "completed" is too final — give the
listener hope of other stuff that might not be shite; never assume
the listener had a good time (gods, they may have waded through a
ventilator of it); keep the exit teaching. Candidates auditioned by
`just moira-epilogue`; the winner replaces the constant.

1. At an end — "This document is at an end. There are others. The
   exit is two zeros."
2. Concluded, vernacular — "This document is concluded. Others
   exist, and some of them might not be shite." (the landing's own
   500ms beat does the timing) "The exit is two zeros."
3. Finished — "This document is finished. Further documents are
   available. The exit is two zeros."
4. Zero zero — "This document is at an end. There are others. The
   exit is zero zero." (the avionics Stop voice — and possibly the
   flatter landing)

## The audio equivalent of shite — the taxonomy

The question was jotted; the answer is in the house's own terms.
Shite is undifferentiated bulk; in audio, undifferentiated means
unpaused. The boring list is the audio shite — information at uniform
density, nothing scheduled, the listener's posterior ignored. The
ventilator is the exact image: continuous flow without breath, which
is literally TTS without breaths.json. The decorated sibling is the
uniformly rising read — prosody sprayed regardless of meaning (the
final-rise note above is a specimen; decorated Stuff, in audio form).
The house already owns the anti-shite instruments: the breaths, the
vernacular landing, the question pause, the and-beat. The pauses are
the shite-filter made audible. The Irish term keeps the older vowel —
the genteel sibling of the American — so its audio equivalent is
equally well-bred: not noise, but low-yield signal at uniform
density. The drone.
