# RHS CHIP TEST XYZ

A short document built to make the right-edge LCARS tab obvious.

## The panel bookend

The left badge and the right chip frame each header as a bounded panel.
Resize the terminal horizontally — the chip should track the right edge
and the wrapped text should stay within it.

## Short header

Even a two-word header gets the chip at the far right column.

## Body is unbounded

Body paragraphs keep an implicit right margin. Only headers carry the
visible bookend. That asymmetry is the panel metaphor: the header is the
structural anchor, the body is the content.
