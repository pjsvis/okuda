# Careful recursion — the bounded context's cliff walk

**Date:** 2026-09-10
**Type:** design note (the opportunity overcompletion of context creates, and the dose rule that governs it)
**Status:** An opinion, persisted. No machinery proposed; the guardrails already exist.

---

## The opportunity

With sufficient overcompletion of context in a silo, careful recursion becomes available — for laughs, and for the cliff-edge thrill. The corpus has been recursing all along without the name: the authoring-playbook entry about short paragraphs *is* short-paragraphed; the specimens exhibit actuates itself; the use-vs-mention note exemplifies its own thesis; the session wrote a rule against the appetite for rules while feeling the appetite.

## Self-application is the coherence test

The bounded context's own law (`docs/2026-09-10-document-types-through-time.md`): *when the context is bounded, contradiction is intolerable and must be resolved.* So apply the doctrine to the corpus itself: if nothing contradicts, the doctrine passed. Every recursion this thread attempted — the style rules applied to the style doc, the FO verdict applied to our own walls, the stop rule applied to the session — passed. The laughs are the proof's sugar coating.

## The cliff is walkable because the constitution removed the drop

The safety is architectural, not lucky. The no-state constitution is accidentally recursion-hostile in exactly the right ways:

- **The depth-one stack is the base case.** Dereferencing cannot descend unboundedly; a loop that cannot loop deep cannot hurt you.
- **The 88 cap is the anti-recursion invariant.** Self-citation inflates until `§98`, then stops; the remedy is editorial.
- **The excursion contract holds your place** through any deliberate Möbius stroll — a cycle costs a window, not your context.
- **The exit is always two zeros.** The one thing self-reference cannot take from you is the door.

The web's recursion is the rabbit hole — unbounded, each cycle a new tab. Ours is bounded by construction: the difference between a paradox and a joke.

## The dose rule

The failure modes name themselves: the *Möbius brief* (cites only itself — decorated Stuff, loop without payload); the registry indexing the registry; the rule about rules about rules — the dogfood appetite at the next power, already gated (`docs/2026-09-10-use-vs-mention.md`).

**One self-application per doctrine, then stop; each recursion earns its place like any other reference.** A recursion that teaches nothing new about the doctrine is a mirror, not a proof.

## The one line

Recursion in a bounded context is a coherence test that terminates; the grammar is the base case, the cap is the invariant, the door is always two zeros — and the session that discussed satisficing at length until overbudget is the demo, and the stop.
