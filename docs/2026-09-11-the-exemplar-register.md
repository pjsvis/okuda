# The exemplar register

Some things are the right sort of thing. Who knows what. The register
collects witnesses: instances noted on sight, dated, one line each, no
definition owed — the category, if it ever firms up, emerges from the
witnesses rather than preceding them. Impressions before ideas. A
resolved definition is welcome to arrive late, or never.

## Entry one — the wine-dark sea

οἶνοψ πόντος. Homer's formula, ~2700 years in service. Two words,
zero configuration, one exact metrical slot — Milman Parry's formulaic
composition: the epithet as the oral tradition's compiled unit, the
bard composing by lookup, in real time, in the dark. Effortless system
invariants; a control surface of two words. The smallest thing with the
longest run-time in the language.

The tension is unresolved, deliberately: the sea is not the color of
wine, and the phrase never claims hue. It behaves like a seventh or
more that never resolves — each century reharmonizes it against its
own theory without changing a word: Gladstone's color-blindness (1858,
a note that outran every resolution), Berlin & Kay's color-term
evolution, the glare hypothesis of low-sun water. A fixed carrier with
an open payload. Zero entropy in form; the meaning-space left
maximally open — which is why every generation resolves it differently
and none has stopped.

And it is the Shannon turn at civilizational scale: the question mark's
pause is the listener's compute time; the wine-dark sea is a
three-thousand-year pause, still computing. The reader tries to guess;
the poem continues with confirmation or new information; the next
century's guess becomes a paper.

Noted 2026-09-11, session's end, en route to Voyager — another crew
waylaid on a wine-dark sea, seven seasons of unresolved tension,
trying to get home.
